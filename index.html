free, open-source photo culling. no import, no subscription. review RAW and JPEG photos and video, then keep the best

download in App Store

or: github, homebrew

Apple silicon · macOS 14 or later

read the walkthrough
  1. open a folder or memory card. Gallery shows one photo at a time
  2. press g to switch to Grid and compare the items
  3. press s to inspect a photo at 100%
  4. mark yes with f or no with d. stars and color labels are separate decisions
  5. filter to yes to see your keepers
  6. open Export to preview the copy: 7 items, 14 RAW+JPEG files. originals stay at the source
