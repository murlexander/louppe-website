privacy policy

Louppe reviews media locally. website analytics are optional

updated 7 October 2026 · Alex Markin · a@alex-markin.com

the Louppe app

Alex Markin makes Louppe, an independent macOS app. it has no account, advertising, app analytics, or tracking SDK. the app sends Alex no media, review decisions, or usage history

your files and review data

Louppe reads chosen folders for previews, playback, metadata, and review. duplicate and similarity analysis runs on your Mac. no media is uploaded to Alex or a cloud analysis service

decisions, star ratings, color labels, filenames, and file identities are saved in .louppe_session.json in the opened folder, with a backup in Louppe’s Application Support data. XMP sidecars and exports go to chosen locations

preferences and recent folder paths stay local. sandboxed builds retain macOS access bookmarks for chosen folders. thumbnail caches speed up review. local operation journals record paths and progress for recovery; destination access is retained while interrupted work may need it

retention and your control

session sidecars, backups, preferences, and cached previews may survive closing or uninstalling Louppe. sidecars and exports remain until you remove them. deleting local app data through macOS may erase review decisions or recovery information. Louppe prunes thumbnails and removes completed operation journals

export copies files by default. moving, renaming, organizing, writing XMP, and sending files to Trash require your chosen actions. cloud or shared-folder services may sync files and sidecars under their own settings and privacy policies

updates and Apple resources

the direct-download app uses Sparkle to check for and download GitHub releases. the host receives connection information, including your IP address. Sparkle system profiling is disabled. turn off automatic checks in Louppe’s Updates settings. Mac App Store builds use Apple updates without Sparkle

if a selected Apple RAW decoder needs model resources, macOS may download them from Apple. decoding stays local. Apple manages downloads under Apple’s privacy policy

contacting Alex

contact and feedback links open your email app. sending is your choice. Alex receives your address, message, and chosen attachments to respond and investigate. email providers handle delivery

correspondence is kept for follow-up and resolution. contact a@alex-markin.com to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you sent. include only attachments you want to share

the louppe.eu website

GitHub Pages receives page requests and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, even without analytics consent. see GitHub’s privacy statement for its retention and processing practices

Google Analytics loads only after “allow analytics” on the HTTPS production site. it measures page views, referring pages, browser and device information, approximate location, and download-link clicks. clicks record intent, not installation or app activity. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled

Google receives analytics requests under Google’s privacy policy. _ga and _ga_* cookies expire after 30 days without renewal. your choice lasts 180 days in browser local storage. if storage is unavailable, it may last only for this visit

reject or withdraw anytime through “analytics choices” in the footer. withdrawal stops collection across open Louppe website tabs and clears analytics cookies. withdrawal does not delete data already received by Google. local previews do not load analytics

downloads, source links, and donations use external services with their own privacy policies. this site has no account, media upload, or contact form

questions and changes

send privacy questions or requests to a@alex-markin.com. changes to privacy practices will appear here with a revised date
